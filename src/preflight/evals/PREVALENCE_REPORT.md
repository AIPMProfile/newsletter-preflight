## Method

- Module A only. The intent reviewer costs money per document and adds no prevalence signal.
- Share is the fraction of **documents** carrying a code at least once, never the fraction of findings.
- Link probing is live unless `--no-link-check` was passed, so `link.broken` reflects the web on the generation date above.
- Raw sources and their provenance are in `real/manifest.json`.
